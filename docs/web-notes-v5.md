# Web notes: Town v5 (information release: finance, ledgers, energy, shopping trips)

Design: `docs/town-v5.md`. Contract: `docs/snapshot-contract.md` section 14 (v1.4). Scope: `web/` only.

## What was built
- **Contract types** (`contract/index.ts`): `AccountRef`, `LedgerKind`, `LedgerEntry`, `LedgerSummaryRow`, `LedgerPage`, `PersonLedger`, `LedgerQuery`, `FinanceView` (+ `FinanceFlows`), the new `EnergyView`, `Snapshot.finance`, `HistoryPoint` v1.4 fields (optional). `SchemaVersion` is `3 | 4`.
- **SimPort**: `ledger(account, query?)` and `personLedger(person)` on the mock and the wasm wrapper (`sim.ledger(accountJson, queryJson)`, `sim.person_ledger(id)`; the names turned out right). The `wasm/upgrade.ts` shim is gone: `loadWasmPort` refuses a package that is not schemaVersion 4 with the new `energy` and `finance` blocks ("older than contract v1.4 ... rebuild with npm run build:wasm") and the app falls back to the mock.
- **Mock ledger** (`sim-port/mock/ledger.ts`, `finance.ts`): INVENTED money, booked as double-entry entries from the mock's own monthly income lines (wages, taxes, dividends, rent, benefits, interest, loan service, purchases split over the sectors, public spending, upkeep, debt interest, borrowing to cover shortfalls). Household savings ARE the household's ledger balance. Detail for the last 8 turns (`DETAIL_TURNS`), older entries rolled up per (account, counterparty, kind, turn, month); opening balances at turn 0; `reconciles` computed from opening + entries + roll-ups = closing. Saved with the mock save (mock schema 4; older saves are refused with a clear message). Finance flows are summed from the same bookings, so the Finance tab and the ledgers agree. Energy demand/supply numbers are invented (Town v5 doc example values).
- **Finance tab** (`FinancePanel.svelte`, `CircularFlow.svelte`, `panels/finance.ts`): Government, Bank (with a capital ratio gauge against the limit), Households, Firms, Money, Prices and distribution; month and trailing-quarter columns, sparklines from history, "ledger >" links (government revenue and spending rows by kind, bank rows, per-household and per-firm buttons), government and bank ledger buttons at the top, circular flow diagram for the last quarter.
- **Ledger viewer**: inspector section `LedgerPreview` (badge, opening, closing, latest five, "Open full ledger") for household, firm, government, bank (clicking the town hall or bank tile now selects them: new `Selection` kinds `government` and `bank`); `LedgerModal` + `LedgerTable` full viewer: filters (kind, period, counterparty), clickable counterparties (select that entity and switch the ledger), running balance, RECONCILIATION badge (recomputed in the viewer; red if either the sim flag or the sum disagrees), roll-up table for older periods, paging (25 rows, PageUp/PageDown inside the table), scroll regions are focusable. A person's inspector shows wages earned, job events and the "people have no purse" line (`PersonEarnings`).
- **Energy panel** (`EnergyPanel.svelte`, `panels/energy.ts`, in the Ecology tab): per-plant table (tile, kind, capacity, share, emissions), demand split firms/households, served share with a rationing warning, intensity, renewable share, unit tooltip (button with `title` and `aria-label`). The plant inspector shows capacity, share of the grid and emissions. Unit is `energy.unit` + "/month".
- **Links**: Metrics (Public debt row -> government ledger), People panel (selected household -> its ledger), Results screen (money events: rent shortfall, bailout, loan default, purchase, spending cut -> the ledger; government and bank ledger links).
- **Scene**: shopping trips (`scene/people.ts` `planShopping`, `planTrips`; `store/scene-energy.ts` for the scene's fossil share). At most 5 figures per month, one per household, non-children not already commuting, biggest real `flows.purchases` first with one per sector if possible; same router and walk as commutes (out, linger, back). Empty or missing purchases give none. Works in playback and Pressure mode (both call `planTrips`).

## Contract issues from before the real wire (resolved: see 'Real wire' above)
1. **`snapshot.energy` reuses a key with a different shape.** Section 14 replaces `EnergyView` (`fossilCap`, `supply: number`, ...) with `{ unit, supply[], demand{}, ... }`. The Rust package built before v1.4 still sends the old one. The web upgrades it in `wasm/upgrade.ts`; if the real v1.4 keeps both shapes under one key the types clash (`supply` is a number in one and an array in the other).
2. **Trailing-quarter totals have no named fields.** Section 14 says "`*Quarter` fields added by the wire agent". The web assumes `finance.quarter?: FinanceFlows` (same flows as the monthly values: `govRevenue`, `govSpending`, `householdIncome`, `householdTaxes`, `rentPaid`, `loanPayments`, `householdSpending`, `firmRevenue`, `wageBill`, `firmProfit`, `investment`, `bankInterestIncome`). Rename in `contract` and `panels/finance.ts` when the real names land; without them the Quarter column says n/a.
3. **Wasm export names for the ledger** are assumed (`ledger(accountJson, queryJson)`, `person_ledger(id)`).
4. `energy.unit` is documented as `'MWh'` while the design says the label is `MWh/month`; the web appends "/month" unless the unit already contains a "/".
5. `query` filters and `reconciles`: the web filters client-side and expects `reconciles` to cover the whole account whatever the query.
6. Nothing in section 14 lists employment events with a turn; `PersonLedger.events` carries bare `SimEvent`s, so the person view cannot date a hire or a firing.

## Real wire (Town v5 integration)
- **Types match the wire**: `LedgerEntry.debtAfter`, `LedgerSummaryRow.net/debtNet`, `LedgerPage.openingDebt/closingDebt`, `PersonLedger.summary/eventTurns`, `FinanceView.government.cash`, `FinanceView.quarter` (one `FinanceFlows`), `SchemaVersion = 4`.
- **Reconciliation rule (viewer, `panels/ledger.ts` `reconcile`)**: for balance AND debt, opening + roll-up `net`/`debtNet` + every entry's effect = closing, and each entry's `balanceAfter`/`debtAfter` equals the running position before it plus its own effect (`entryEffect`: transfer; loan_new raises borrower balance and debt, the bank only its loans; loan_repay lowers both; loan_writeoff; bailout and unpaid interest add public debt and bank equity and loans). Red if the sim flag, either sum or any entry chain disagrees. `tests/helpers.ts` `reconciles` is a second, independently written copy; `tests/wasm-real.test.ts` plays 12 turns on the real wasm sim and asserts every household, firm, government and bank page passes both, finance totals equal ledger sums by kind for the latest month, the quarter equals three months, energy is consistent.
- **Display**: a Debt change and Debt (Loans for the bank) column appear when an account has any debt; bank-as-bystander loan rows show "no cash" and the borrower as counterparty; "Bank credit creation/destruction" are shown in italics with a plain-words tooltip (no ledger link); memos are rebuilt from entity names (the wire memo says "household 3"); the bank page has a one-line explanation of equity and lending. Person page: job events carry their turn ("Jan to Mar 2027: ...") from `eventTurns`, older earnings (roll-up) are summed.
- **Firm revenue**: ledger revenue is money received and can sit up to ~10% below the model's firm revenue (the Factory buying capital goods from itself moves no money). A short note says so in the Finance tab (Firms section, tooltip on Revenue) and in firm ledgers (inspector and viewer).
- **Finance before the first month**: flows show "not yet played" instead of 0. Person events are dated and the mock keeps their turn.
- **Mock**: same shapes and rule (debt tracked per account from loan_new bookings, bank loans rise as a bystander, roll-up rows carry `net`/`debtNet`, `quarter` always present, `government.cash`).

## Contract issues (real wire)
1. Summary rows carry gross `inflow`/`outflow` and the effect `net`/`debtNet`; for a bystander (the bank in a loan) `inflow` and `outflow` are not the account's cash, so the viewer uses `net` and `debtNet` only.
2. The effect of an entry on the open account is not on the wire (no `posting`); the viewer infers it from kind, detail and the two ends (table above). If a new posting appears (a detail text changes) the badge goes red rather than silently wrong. Asking for `posting` or a per-entry `effect` on the entry would remove the inference.
3. Real numbers worth a look (model, not UI): a landlord household shows savings near $1k with $110k of debt at the start; the owner household pays capital tax of about 40% of its monthly capital income.
