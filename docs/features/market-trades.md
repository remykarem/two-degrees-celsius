# Feature: Market trades (the list behind each market)

Status: **APPROVED by the user, IMPLEMENTED (contract v2.4; see "As built")** (request: "see the ENTIRE LIST behind each market: the individual purchases and sales with units and price"). Builds on the Markets view (`docs/features/markets.md`, contract v2.3). Contract **v2.4**, additive. No simulation behaviour change: the state hash must stay byte-identical (`cargo run -p sim-cli --example statehash` before and after). Where this doc and the code disagree, the code wins.

## 1. Why
The Markets view shows only aggregates ("545 units bought, price $23"). A learner cannot see who bought what from whom. Each market card gets a **See all trades** list: one row per trade, with the people and firms as clickable references, units and prices, and a totals row that adds up to the number on the card.

## 2. What a "trade" is, per market (checked against the code)
| Market | Row | Columns | Counted in the totals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food, Goods, Services | one purchase: a buyer (household, a firm buying capital goods, or the government) from a shop | Buyer, Seller, Units, Unit price, Total, Month | every row (units add up to the card's "bought") |
| Labour | one job: a person and an employer, plus open jobs and jobs lost | Worker, Employer, Wage a month, What changed | firm jobs (the card's "traded"); job guarantee, domestic staff, fired and open jobs are listed but not counted, and say so |
| Housing | one home: who lives there, who owns it, the rent | Home, Lives there, Owner, Rent a month, Status | occupied homes (the card's "housed"); vacant homes and homeless households are listed, not counted |
| Credit | one loan request of a firm: asked, approved, rate | Borrower, Lender, Asked for, Approved, Rate | approved dollars (the card's "traded") |
| Energy | not listed: the model has one town-wide supply and demand, no per-agent energy flows (verified: `State.energy` holds aggregates only), so the card says so | none | n/a |

## 3. Design decisions
1. **Capture, not state.** Per-trade detail is output only. Shops' purchases get a new `TraceRecord::Trade` pushed by `goods.rs` (pure addition to the already output-only trace; nothing reads it, so the hash cannot change). Labour, housing and credit rows are built in the wrapper from the state after each tick plus the tick's hire/fire events and the existing `CreditDecision` trace.
2. **Storage.** A `TradeStore` inside the API wrapper's `LedgerStore` (same clone-on-turn, same save blob, same `detail_turns`). Full rows for the last `ledger.detail_turns` turns. Older turns are rolled up per (market, buyer, seller, other party, note, turn) with exact unit and money totals; the month and the per-month price are dropped (unit price becomes total money over total units). Saved with the game; the field defaults to empty, so older saves still load (no `SCHEMA_VERSION` bump; the list simply starts at the load point).
3. **Units without drift.** A buyer's units are the shop's sold units times that buyer's share of the shop's revenue, so the rows add up to the card's "bought" exactly (up to float error). Money is the exact cents the ledger moved.
4. **Wire.** `marketTrades(market, query?)` (wasm `market_trades`) returns `MarketTrades`: period chosen, the periods available, `columns` (id, label, plain-English help, numeric), `rows` of `cells` (`text`, optional `sort` number, optional `entity` reference), a `totals` row, the card's own aggregate and `reconciles`. All labels and help come from `content/ui.json` (`markets.list[].trades`, `text.markets.trades_*`); a new market needs content and a Rust builder, and **no web change**.
5. **UI.** A **See all trades** button on each market card opens a modal: month/turn selector, sortable column headers (client-side only), the totals row, the reconcile line. Entities link to the inspector. Household and firm inspectors list the trades they took part in this month (the same call, with an `entity` filter).
6. **Recorded demo.** Like the ledger, the replay port refuses (`unsupported`), and the button says the list needs the live simulation. Measured in section 5.
7. No bare percentages anywhere in the new text; rates are "dollars a year for every $100 borrowed".

## 4. Tests
Rust: reconcile (rows' counted units and money equal the card's `traded`, and food money equals the ledger's purchase total) across seeds and all 40 turns and live months; roll-up keeps totals exact; save/load round trip; determinism (same seed, same trades). Web: model/sort/format; a made-up market renders from the wire alone. State hash identical.


## As built (differences from the draft above, and what was measured)
- **Code:** `crates/sim-types/src/enums.rs` (`TraceRecord::Trade`, `TradeBuyer`), `crates/sim-model/src/goods.rs` (the push), `crates/sim-api/src/trades.rs` (stored rows, roll-up, `MarketTrades` and its parts, the builder), a `trades` field of `LedgerStore` (`ledger.rs`), `Api::market_trades` and wasm `market_trades`. Content: `content/ui.json` `markets.trades` (shared words) and `markets.list[].trades` (intro, empty text, columns with help, notes, totals sentence), validated at load against the closed id sets `trade_column_ids` / `trade_note_ids` in `markets.rs`. Web: `panels/trades.ts` (sort, period, reconcile line), `TradesTable.svelte`, `TradesModal.svelte`, `EntityTrades.svelte` (inspector), the button in `MarketStrip.svelte`, `store` `tradesView` / `openTrades` / `marketTrades`.
- **No `SCHEMA_VERSION` bump** (stays 7). The saved ledger gained a `trades` field that defaults to empty, so a schema 7 save from before this feature still loads (tested); its trade list starts where it was loaded. The wasm golden changed on purpose in two hashes only: `catalog` (new content text) and `export` (the save now carries trades); every per-turn hash and the state hash are unchanged (`cargo run -p sim-cli --example statehash` identical before and after, all rows).
- **A firm can buy its own output** (the Factory buying machines from itself). Those units are part of the card's "bought" and appear as rows with the note "A firm buying its own output: no money moves"; they are why a list's money can exceed what the ledger moved (the test adds them back). The same fact is behind the ledger note in `finance.ts` (`f-rev`).
- **Entity references** gained `person`, `government`, `bank` (`MarketEntityKind`), so a worker, the bank and the government are links too. A worker's text carries the household ("Adult 1 (Owner family)") because person labels repeat.
- **Periods:** every turn can be shown as a whole (three months summed) and the last `detail_turns` turns also month by month. Older turns exist only as sums per (market, parties, note, turn), with `count` = months seen; their unit price is total money over total units, wages and rents are per month worked, and the "When" column says "3 of the 3 months". Because a trade is already one row per pair per month, the roll-up saves a third of the rows, not more (decision: roll up per turn, not per month, for that reason).
- **Reconcile:** `MarketTrades.reconciles` is set for the latest month with nothing filtered out: goods and credit compare counted units (credit: approved dollars), labour and housing compare counted rows (firm jobs, occupied homes) with the card's `traded`. The Rust test runs it for 6 markets, 5 seeds, every turn and the live months; it also checks that the three product lists' money equals what the ledger moved (plus own-output purchases), that a whole turn is its three months, that rolled totals are bit-exact in money and within 1e-6 in units, and that a save round-trips. Energy has no list (aggregates only; the card says so).
- **Replay demo:** no list (the replay port refuses, like the ledger); the button opens a window saying so. Adding per-month lists to the recording would grow it by about 6 markets x 120 months x 7 KB.
- **Measured** (seed 42, 40 turns, native release build; wasm in the web test after 12 turns): one market's list for one month is 5 to 8 KB of JSON (about 5 KB of it is column help and intro text repeated in every reply), all six markets 42.5 KB native or 37 KB through wasm; `market_trades` costs 74 microseconds natively and 0.32 ms per call through wasm including JSON parse, against 97 microseconds for a snapshot. The snapshot itself is unchanged (92 KB). The store at turn 40 holds 1,035 rows and 820 sums; the save blob is 1.48 MB, of which trades are 344 KB (23%). Nothing is sent unless the player opens a list or selects a household, firm or person (six small calls, one per market, only for non-empty ones).
- **Not verified:** a narrow or mobile layout (checked at 1440 wide only); screen-reader behaviour of the sort headers (they are buttons with `aria-sort`); a very long game's list in the browser beyond turn 10; seeds other than 7 in the browser (other seeds are covered by the Rust test).
